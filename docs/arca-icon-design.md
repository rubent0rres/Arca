# Icono de Arca

## Concepto

Un cofre violeta con documentos, inspirado en el significado de Arca como recipiente que guarda y protege archivos. Se mantiene la paleta violeta de la aplicación y un fondo transparente fuera del contorno del icono.

## Archivos

- `public/arca-icon.png`: PNG de 1024 × 1024 con transparencia, utilizado en la interfaz.
- `build/arca.icns`: icono de macOS con resoluciones de 16 a 1024 píxeles.

## Generación

Generado con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`. El PNG original se conserva en la carpeta de imágenes generadas de Codex. La copia del proyecto se adaptó a 1024 × 1024 y a los tamaños estándar de macOS conservando la transparencia.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final square macOS application icon for Arca, an archive manager for RAR, 7z and ZIP files.
Primary request: a distinctive modern coffer / storage chest that conveys keeping digital files together. A single compact chest with a gently arched lid, two clean subtle structural bands, and one simple light lavender clasp on the front. The lid is very slightly ajar, showing just a few broad pale file edges inside. The silhouette should read as a chest, not a shipping box or briefcase.
Style: refined contemporary macOS app icon, sculpted satin 3D forms with restrained depth, smooth surfaces and crisp edges, elegant and friendly. Broad graphic forms that remain recognizable at 32px. Nearly frontal view with a tiny amount of visible top and right side.
Composition: square canvas 1024x1024; one deep plum/violet rounded-square squircle tile, centered with about 9% transparent margin on every side; a single large chest centered on the tile, filling about 70% of tile width. Tile subtly illuminated behind the chest; chest in rich violet with lighter lavender highlights and pale ivory-lavender file edges and clasp. Controlled soft lighting from upper left, restrained short shadows wholly inside the tile. Seamless cohesive icon, no surrounding scene.
Constraints: truly transparent outside the squircle, clean anti-aliased alpha edges. No text, no letters, no words, no watermark, no multiple icons, no icon grid, no frame or mockup. No wood texture, no treasure coins, no pirate details, no ship, no excessive glow, no complicated tiny decorations. Deliver only the production-ready icon.
